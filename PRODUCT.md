# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Residents who need to report an emergency without creating an account and later track the report through a private link.
- MDRRMO staff who review reports, set status and priority, and communicate with reporters.
- MDRRMO administrators who oversee staff, configuration, and audit information.

## Product Purpose

An account-free emergency reporting, response-coordination, and tracking service for the MDRRMO. It collects useful report details, communicates verified response updates, and helps residents stay informed. Anyone in immediate danger must still call the local emergency hotline.

## Positioning

Guided, structured intake with private seven-day tracking, staff follow-up, and accountable status history.

## Operating Context

Residents may use the service on low-end phones and unreliable mobile connections, often from rural locations described by landmarks. Staff use protected operational screens to triage and follow up on reports. Public reporting is unauthenticated; staff and administrators sign in.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing report, tracking, messaging, geolocation, photo, staff triage, and administration behavior.
- Preserve the urgent-hotline warning and clearly state that a submitted report does not mean responders were dispatched; only a verified administrator dispatch update may make that claim.
- Preserve current language support and MDRRMO identity assets; the visual treatment may be refreshed.
- Keep public screens responsive, low-bandwidth-conscious, and usable on low-end phones.
- Never expose private contact details, internal notes, or restricted evidence on public tracking screens.

## Brand Commitments

The product is the MDRRMO Emergency Response Portal. Keep the existing Kalibo/MDRRMO identity assets, while allowing their visual treatment to be redesigned.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product requirements and operational constraints: `prd.md`.
- Existing logo and local-government imagery are in `public/assets/`.
- Do not invent dispatch claims, response guarantees, or operational metrics; administrators may record verified ambulance or response-group dispatch notices.

## Product Principles

- Make urgent reporting and the hotline distinction unmistakable.
- Collect the most useful incident information early.
- Be honest about submission, status, connectivity, and tracking expiry.
- Protect reporter privacy while making follow-up legible.
- Keep operational actions clear and accountable.
